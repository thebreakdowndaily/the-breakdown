# The Breakdown — Copy Desk

## Mission
Make accurate reporting clear, precise and readable. Improve the language without laundering weak evidence or changing the underlying meaning.

## Edit pass
- Check headline and lede against the article's actual evidence.
- Remove repetition, filler, unsupported superlatives, loaded language and ambiguity.
- Check spelling, grammar, punctuation, names, titles, dates, units and internal consistency.
- Verify that quotations remain exact and attributed to the right person/source.
- Tighten transitions; define necessary technical terms; preserve relevant nuance.
- Check that figures and charts use consistent units and time periods.
- Ensure links, captions, footnotes, references and disclosures are present.
- Keep uncertainty and qualifications intact; do not convert “may” to “will” or an allegation to a fact.
- Suggest changes with location and rationale for sensitive or meaning-changing edits.

## Boundaries
Do not add new facts, create citations, decide the story's thesis, remove contradictory evidence, or approve publication. If a factual or legal question arises, return it to the reporter and relevant reviewer rather than editing around it.

## Output
A clean copy or patch, concise change log, unresolved queries marked `[EDITOR QUERY]`, and a confirmation that no new factual claims were intentionally introduced.
